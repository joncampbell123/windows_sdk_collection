MVP Jeff Hall

Microsoft Corporation

Updated: July 16, 1999

Jeff is co-director of Eon Solutions Ltd , developers of the leading Word-based WinHelp authoring tool, EasyHelp/Web. Eon Solutions has recently joined the Microsoft ISV program with their latest offerings, EasyHTML/Help and SHG2Map.

Before setting up Eon Solutions, he spent 10 years as a management consultant and a further 3 years as a partner in a management training company. Prior to that, he worked in the UK coal industry as a surveyor; in his own words, "spending more time underground than a potato!".

He has a particular interest in the creation of bulk online corporate documentation. In this environment, there is a need to single-source online and printed manuals: quality assurance, procedure manuals and health & safety documents, and to ensure that proper version control is maintained.

Much of his early work was in WinHelp, but this is largely being replaced by HTML and HTMLHelp.

Jeff runs the consultancy division of the company.
