MVP Scott Boggan

Microsoft Corporation

Updated: April 7, 1999

Scott Boggan is co-author of the award-winning Developing Online Help for Windows and the forthcoming HTML Help: A Design & Strategy Guide (O'Reilly & Associates). He is a popular speaker at numerous conferences and also teaches through the University of Washington. He recently founded HelpCraft, a consulting and training company.

Scott's first hypertext project was documenting a DOS TSR program, and after that experience he fairly swooned over WinHelp when he began working with it in 1990. Once upon a time Scott was part of the Seattle grunge music scene, and he still finds time to play guitar (sans amplification).
