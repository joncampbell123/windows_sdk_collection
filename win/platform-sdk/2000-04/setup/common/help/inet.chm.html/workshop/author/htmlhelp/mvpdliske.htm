MVP David Liske

Microsoft Corporation

Updated: July 16, 1999

David Liske has been designing Help files since 1994, and has been documenting computer systems since 1976. He is the owner of Delmar Computing Services , providing consulting, training, software development, and web site design. An author and technical reviewer for Wrox Press, he's performed technical review of Beginning Access 97 VBA Programming and a number of other books. His experience includes working as a calibration systems technician and programmer for the U.S. Navy, and developing software, designing databases, and developing PLC-based robotics systems for the Toyota Technical Center.

David's HTMLHelp Center at VB Explorer specializes in HTML Help techniques and tools for Visual Basic, VBA, and Delphi developers. He lives in lower Michigan, sharing a converted 1851 one-room schoolhouse with his wife Marnella, their four homeschooled children, and a daily average of three computers. The Liske family web site is at http://users.tc3net.com/delmar .
