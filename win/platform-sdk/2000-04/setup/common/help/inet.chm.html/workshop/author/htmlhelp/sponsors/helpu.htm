At Help University, we are committed to bringing the latest technologies to our conferences and training events. HTML Help, along with other HTML-based online communications, represents our newest challenge as an industry, and we are looking forward to exploring the frontier of this new online communication technology from Microsoft.

Alec Sonenthal, President of Help University, Inc.


Help University, Inc., a group of training, consulting, and management professionals, is dedicated to furthering the interests and growth of the community of WinHelp and HTML developers while serving the individual needs of its clients. The focus of Help University is to facilitate learning and networking among online communications developers specializing in WinHelp and Web-based information through its Web site, conferences, and HelpNet courses.

Through its conferences and training classes (offered through HelpNet), Help University offers HTML Help training classes, workshops, and presentations from the best online communication professionals around the world.
