MVP MJ Plaster

Microsoft Corporation

Updated: March 8, 1999

President of World Wide WInnovations, Inc. M.J. Plaster is also Executive Vice President of Help University, Inc., an HTML-based Help and WinHelp training, consulting and conference firm. Help University co-produces the Info Online Conference, and co-sponsors the HTML Help Business Development Program.

A certified Doc-To-Help, HDK, and RoboHELP trainer, she is proficient in many WinHelp and HTML development tools and third-party add-ons. She is also one of the first WexTech MVPs. A member of the Society for Technical Communications, National Writers Union, and HTML Writers Guild, and charter member of HelpNet, she has specialized in WinHelp development for the past seven years. More recently she has begun HTML Help development, and brings many years of technical writing, training and training development, as well as newspaper feature writing and business management experience to World Wide WInnovations. In her spare time, she works as a member of the Tennessee Victims' Coalition.
