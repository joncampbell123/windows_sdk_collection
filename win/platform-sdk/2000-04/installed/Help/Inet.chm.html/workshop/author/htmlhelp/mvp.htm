HTML Help MVP Program

Microsoft Corporation

March 8, 1999

The HTML Help Most Valuable Professional (MVP) Program is designed to recognize professionals who voluntarily give their expertise to enhance the online experience and technical skills of others. The charter members of this program are:

  • Josef Becker, Becker & Poettgen Software GmbH
  • Scott Boggan, HelpCraft
  • Robert Chandler, Varian
  • Dana Cline, CompuServe HYPERTEXT forum sysop
  • Mary Deaton, KNOWware
  • Jeff Hall, Eon Solutions
  • David Liske, Delmar Computing Services
  • Cheryl Lockett Zubak, WorkWrite
  • Paul Neshamkin, The Paul Neshamkin Group
  • Paul O'Rear, Sageline Software
  • M.J. Plaster, WWWinnovations

The Microsoft MVPs are active members of he WinHlp-L mailing list and CompuServe HYPERTEXT forum. You can reach them by signing up for these services.

To learn more about Microsoft's MVP Program and how MVPs help, visit the MVP Web site .
