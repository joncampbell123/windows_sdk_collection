MVP Josef Becker

Microsoft Corporation

Updated: March 8, 1999

Josef Becker is Managing Director of Becker & Poettgen Software GmbH, a firm that is specialized in developing CBT (Computer Based Training), WBT (Web Based Training) and all kind of HBT (Hypertext Based Training). The firm is located in Germany, customers are banking institutes and ministries in the German speaking countries.

Josef wrote his first technical documentations in 1982. Ten years later he wrote his first WinHelp system (the hard way - using pure Word). Since that time he is fascinated by everthing that jumps: WinHelp, HTML, HTML Help.

In 1989, 1992 and 1995 he was awarded by IFCEB (International Foundation for Computer Based Training in Banking & Finance) for his outstanding training materials.

You can find out more about him from his Web site at http://www.helpmaster.com
