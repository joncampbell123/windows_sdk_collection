Publications

The Microsoft Press site has book listings you can view by title or by subject.

15 Seconds , a twice monthly online publication written for Internet developers and content providers about IIS, ISAPI, and Active Server.

Training

MSDN Training area features online training presentations, live chats with technology experts, interactive tutorials, and streamlined links to courses, CD-ROM training, peer support, books, and certification.

Microsoft Training and Certification Program provides training and certification options for Microsoft products and technologies, for users ranging from beginners to experts, and from systems professionals to professional developers.

Microsoft Mastering Series, Internet and Web development provides content-rich, self-paced, interactive training tools designed to help you quickly Web authoring and development with Microsoft tools and technologies.

DevelopMentor offers in-depth training on all aspects of Windows programming, and comprehensive coverage of COM, ActiveX, Win32®, MFC, and more.

Gestalt training provides computer training in a hands-on classroom setting on topics ranging from Windows and Macintosh applications to the complete Microsoft Certification program.

Information Management Group (IMG) , provides training on Microsoft developer tools and network technologies, including VBScript, FrontPage, Internet Explorer, Visual InterDev, Visual FoxPro, and Visual J++.
