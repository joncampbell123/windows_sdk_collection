Chapter 4

We have found that a popular question is how to change the contents of two frames at once when clicking on a hyperlink or when using an ActiveX control. We created a sample that captures the ONCLICK event and uses a script to change two of the three frames currently being displayed. We wanted our sample to have the look and feel of a book, displaying two pages at once, as if you had the book laid out in front of you.
