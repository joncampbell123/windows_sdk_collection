Example of the onbeforecut Event

Steps to demonstrate the onbeforecut event:

  1. Select the text, "Select and Cut this Text", in the light-blue SPAN, avoiding extra spaces.
  2. Right-click the mouse and choose Cut from the shortcut menu.
  3. Switch focus to the violet DIV that says, "Paste the Text Here", select that text, and then choose Paste from the shortcut menu.

The onbeforecut event is necessary for enabling the Cut option on the shortcut menu.
