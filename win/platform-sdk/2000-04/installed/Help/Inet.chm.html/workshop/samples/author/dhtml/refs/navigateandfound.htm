The following is a description of Seattle's persnickety rain.

Beyond the jokes, Seattle's rain is quite tepid. The comedic sycophants only suffuse indignant blasphemes of this Emerald City's weather.

The truth: the rain is but a metaphor for our rich liquid sunshine that falls upon our lives, and nourishes us with its righteousness.

Although, the last few years have been noticeably different. The rain is gray and chilly; perhaps clothing has thinned and grown shoddy as the new millenium approaches. And then, I do not recall when the puddles went from being personal play spaces to mere nuisances that must be avoided. All of this is due to that single Seattle rain shower that struck one nigh so very long ago. It crept into our lives and we realized something was lost.

But now and again, the rains-of-old come and we feel right again.
