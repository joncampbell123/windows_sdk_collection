save Snapshot Behavior Sample

This is a sample of the saveSnapshot behavior. This sample provides two demonstrations:

  1. Persist information directly in the form fields.
  2. Persist information within a script block.

For scenarios that would require only completion of the form, the first option would be the best (and easiest) to implement. However, for more involved scenarios that may require field calculations (such as tax forms), storing the information in a persisting script variable may prove to be faster and easier in the long run.
